Education Notes is a student-focused platform built with one simple purpose — to make quality educational resources easier to access, understand and use.

01 — Who We Are

Built for students.

Education Notes is an independent educational platform created to bring useful and well-organised study resources together in one place.


We focus on keeping learning material clear, practical and easy to understand — helping students spend less time searching and more time learning.

"Education becomes powerful when knowledge is simple enough to understand and easy enough to access."

02 — The Founder

Sarthak Singh

Founder & Creator

Education Notes was created by Sarthak Singh with the vision of building a useful and accessible platform for students.


From organising study material to developing the website, the platform represents an effort to combine education, technology and creativity into something genuinely useful for students.
